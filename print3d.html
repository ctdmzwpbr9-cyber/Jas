    Printing in Bambu Studio
    1. Unzip the download. On an iPhone, tap the zip in the Files app.
    2. In Bambu Studio, choose File > Import, then pick the .3mf. If it asks, choose to load geometry only.
    3. Every letter is a separate object, named with its AMS slot, for example "O - slot 1 Pink". Select each one and set its filament to that slot number.
    4. Press A to arrange the plate, then slice. The letters lie flat side down, so they need no supports.
    5. Suggested settings: 0.16 mm layers, 2 to 3 walls and 10 to 15% gyroid infill.
    6. For fuzzy or fluffy letters, also turn on Fuzzy Skin for outer walls (Quality > Others). It roughens the sides and adds to the texture.
